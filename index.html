Synesthesia

One point in a big parameter space makes both the sound and the picture. You steer the search:

  • 👍 More of this — keep going in this direction (smaller steps).
  • 👎 Not this — go back to the last thing you liked and try another direction (bigger steps).
  • 🎲 Surprise — jump near a random built-in preset.
  • ↩ Undo — revert the last change (up to 5).
  • 💾 Save keeps the point in this browser — saved points sit at the top of the list, and 🗑 removes the selected one. 🔗 Share copies a short link. The current point also survives a page reload.

Sound and image share the same slow LFOs, and the picture listens: it breathes with loudness swells, flashes and sprouts new growth (with a ripple) on every hit, and bass / mid / treble tint its dark / mid / light tones. Press ▶ Sound to start audio (browsers need a click for that).
